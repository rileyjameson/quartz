## Claude (3.7 Sonnet)
My best friend for coding questions. Not as good as GPT models at actually explaining what to do, but noticeably better with code. The limits even with the pro version are extremely frustrating though.
### Extended
Hit my limits less and doesn't require me to say "Continue" as often for it to finish writing the code. I find the code isn't as reliable if you have a continuation.
## ChatGPT (4o)
Great for writing. Give it a stream of consciousness thought dump and ask it to make it clear, concise and organized, then iterate from there. Saves me an incredible amount of time during the week.
## Cursor
Auto-complete and chat are incredible. I feel like I'm scratching the surface here but I love it. 
## Superwhisper
I keep on forgetting to use this speech to text tool. Going to need more time.
